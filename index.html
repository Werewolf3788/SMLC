Clay County

Southeastern Illinois | Est. 1824

Nestled in southeastern Illinois, Clay County is a community rich in history and culture. With Louisville as its county seat, it boasts welcoming towns like Louisville, Flora, Xenia, Clay City, and Sailor Springs, offering a unique blend of small-town charm and economic potential.

Our Mission: To highlight the county's significance and promote local business growth, ensuring a prosperous future for all residents.
