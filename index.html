Clay County

Southeastern Illinois | Est. 1842

Nestled in southeastern Illinois, Clay County is a community rich in history and culture. With Louisville as its county seat, it boasts a population of 13,288. Known for its welcoming towns like Louisville, Flora, Xenia, Clay City, and Sailor Springs, Clay County offers a unique blend of small-town charm and economic potential.

Our Mission: To highlight the county's significance and promote local business growth, ensuring a prosperous future for all residents.

Clay County, IL: Quick Answers

Support My Local Community is the Clay County, Illinois community hub. It covers Clay City, Flora, Louisville, Sailor Springs and Xenia with upcoming events, local news, gas prices, weather and a business directory, all in Central Time.

What is Support My Local Community?

Support My Local Community (SMLC) is a free community portal for Clay County, Illinois. It gathers local events, news, weather, gas prices and businesses for Clay City, Flora, Louisville, Sailor Springs and Xenia.

Which towns are in Clay County, Illinois?

This site covers the Clay County communities of Clay City, Flora, Louisville, Sailor Springs and Xenia. Louisville is the county seat, and Flora is the largest city.

What time zone is Clay County, Illinois in?

Clay County, Illinois is in the Central Time Zone. All event times and clocks on this site are shown in Central Time.

Where can I find events in Clay County, IL?

Each town page lists upcoming Clay County events with Central Time start times and calendar links. To add an event for free, use www.supportmylocalcommunity.com/submit-event.

How do I find businesses in Clay County, IL?

Use the Clay County Business Directory at clay-county-business-directory.supportmylocalcommunity.com to find local businesses across the county.
