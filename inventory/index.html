Free home inventory app for your whole household

Home Inventory keeps one live list of everything in your house: groceries, cleaning supplies, toiletries, paper goods and more. Scan barcodes as things come home, scan them out when they're used up or expired, and the app builds your shopping list for you. Everyone you live with shares the same list. It is free from Support My Local Community.

Questions & answers

Is the Home Inventory app free?

Yes. It is free with no ads and nothing to install. Open it in your phone's browser and sign in with Google, Discord or an email address. You can also add it to your home screen like an app.

How do I keep track of what's in my house?

Tap Scan in and scan the barcode on each item as it comes home. The name, brand and picture fill in automatically. When something is used up, empty or expired, tap Scan out and it moves to your shopping list.

Can my roommates or family use the same list?

Yes. Start a household, then share the invite link or the 6-letter code. Everyone who joins sees the same list, and changes show up for everyone right away, including who added or used what.

Is a product's barcode the same at every store?

Yes. A product's barcode (UPC) comes from the company that makes it, so the same product has the same barcode at every store. A different size, count or brand has a different barcode. Store-printed labels on meat and deli items change with each package, so add those by hand.

What if a barcode isn't found?

Type what it is once. The label is saved, so the next scan of that barcode in this app fills in by itself, for you and for everyone else who uses it.

Can I scan a whole case, like a 24-pack?

Yes. Scan the sealed case and mark it as a case with how many are inside. If the case is already opened, enter how many are missing. Single items from it count toward the same total, so a 24-pack plus 3 singles is 27, and each one you scan out takes 1 off.

Does it warn me before food expires?

Yes. Add an expiration or best-by date when you scan something in. The Expiring tab lists what goes bad this week and what has already expired, and expired items can be moved to the shopping list in one tap.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The list is saved on your phone, so you can check it with no signal. Changes sync to your household as soon as you're back online.
