About Sailor Springs

Sailor Springs, IL was established in 1869 around natural mineral water springs. Once a famed regional health resort destination, it remains a tranquil and treasured community in Clay County.

Resort Era & Nature

Sailor Springs' Golden Era

A peaceful haven with a rich history of mineral waters and community preservation.

Sailor Springs, Illinois, attracted visitors from across the nation during the late 19th and early 20th centuries for its restorative mineral waters.

Today, the village honors its resort history while offering a serene landscape and a close community bond for its local residents.
