Put SMLC on your phone

Clay County news, events, gas prices, weather and the business directory in one tap. It installs straight from this website (no app store, nothing to pay) and keeps working when you have no signal.

✓ SMLC is already installed on this device. Open it from your home screen or app list.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open supportmylocalcommunity.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On iPhone it's at the bottom of the screen; on iPad it's at the top right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
  5. The SMLC icon is now on your home screen. Tap it to open the app.

Using Chrome on iPhone? Tap Share at the top right, then Add to Home Screen. On older iPhones, use Safari.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open supportmylocalcommunity.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the app above, or the Install pop-up at the bottom of the screen.
  3. No button? Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Tap Install. The SMLC icon appears with your other apps.

Samsung Internet: tap the menu ☰, then Add page to → Home screen.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Open supportmylocalcommunity.com in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click Install the app above, or the small install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. SMLC opens in its own window and gets a desktop / Start menu shortcut.

Safari on a Mac: choose File → Add to Dock.
