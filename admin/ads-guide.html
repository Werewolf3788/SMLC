1. How Google Ads charges an advertiser

It's not one or the other. When an advertiser sets up a campaign, they pick what they pay for. These are the four main ways.

CPC

Pay per click

The ad can show thousands of times for free. The advertiser pays only when someone clicks it.

Most common. Standard for Google Search ads.

CPM

Pay per 1,000 views

Paid for every 1,000 times the ad is shown, click or not. "Viewable CPM" only counts it if it was actually on screen.

Display/banner ads where the goal is being seen. Billboards work like this.

CPA

Pay per action

Paid when someone does something specific: a purchase, a sign-up, a phone call. Google sets the bids to hit a target cost.

Needs conversion tracking on the advertiser's own site.

CPV

Pay per video view

Paid when someone watches about 30 seconds of a video ad (or all of it, if shorter) or clicks it.

YouTube ads.

How the price is set: an auction

Every time an ad spot opens up, advertisers' bids compete. Google weighs each bid against how good and relevant the ad is, so a better ad can win the spot while paying less than a bigger bidder.

The other side: AdSense (the site gets paid)

SMLC already has AdSense set up (ads.txt and the AdSense script on the directory, gallery and share-photos pages). With AdSense it's reversed: Google puts other companies' ads on the site and pays SMLC, mostly per click, with a small amount per view.

3. Should "partners" be renamed to "ads"?

No. Keep it as "partners." Your instinct was right. Ad blockers decide what to hide mostly by names in the code and addresses: class names, IDs, folder names and file names with words like ad, ads, banner, sponsor, promo, standard ad sizes like 300x250, and known ad servers like Google's. Renaming to "ads" would make ad blockers start hiding the cards.

What the SMLC code does now (checked)

  • Cards use community-partner-card, partner-logo-asset, partners-horizontal-strip. Safe.
  • Partner data comes from SMLC's own Firebase, not an ad network. Safe.
  • Images load from skventuresigns.com/images/. Safe, as long as file names stay neutral (see the check below).
  • The lightbox label "Local Sponsor" is visible text, not a class name. Ad blockers don't hide it, and it's good honest labeling.

The catch: tracking still gets blocked

The cards still show with an ad blocker on, but the view and click counts go through Google Analytics (googletagmanager.com), and most ad blockers and Brave block that. So the GA numbers will be lower than the real number of people who saw a partner.

That's fine for flat monthly pricing. It's a problem only if Scott ever bills per click. If that happens, counts should be stored in SMLC's own database instead.

Rules to keep it safe

  • Never name image files or folders ad-…, …_ad.png, banner…, sponsor…, /ads/.
  • Avoid ad sizes in file names (300x250, 728x90).
  • Never add a class or ID with those words to the partner section.
