About Flora

Flora, IL was founded in 1854 alongside the expansion of the Ohio and Mississippi Railroad and has grown to become the largest city in Clay County. Known for its rich railroad history, beautiful parks, and thriving local business community.

Charley Brown Park

Flora is home to iconic landmarks including the historic B&O Railroad Depot and the expansive Charley Brown Park, serving as regional gathering spots for festivals and community events.

Heart of Clay County

Exploring the enduring legacy, rail history, and community spirit driving Flora forward.

From its roots as a vital railroad junction, Flora has built a lasting reputation as an industrious and welcoming community. Generations of families have shaped the town's character through local commerce, annual celebrations, and continuous civic engagement.

Today, local initiatives and digital networks like Support My Local Community continue to spotlight small businesses, entrepreneurs, and neighborhood programs that make Flora a wonderful place to live and work.
